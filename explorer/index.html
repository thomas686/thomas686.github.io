A look inside 16,591 AI instruction files

What do people make their AI do?

Developers who work with an AI assistant leave it a note: a file called CLAUDE.md or AGENTS.md that the AI reads before every job. It says what work to take on, which rules to follow and what it must never do.

I collected 16,591 of these notes from public projects on GitHub and sorted every line into those three groups. This page shows what people actually ask for.

Which instructions travel together

Each star is one kind of instruction. Bigger stars appear in more files. A line joins two stars when they often sit in the same file, so tight clusters are instructions people tend to give together. Open a star to see what is inside: it bursts into dots, one for each real line, and a list you can search.
